## Original material

The following are original to this project and are not derived from the sources above:

- The title, all interface text, captions, descriptions, transmissions, sigil vocabulary, and credits.
- The sigil engine and its generated marks.
- The section layouts, broken grid, typography treatments, red marks, stamps, seals, and registration details.
- The cutting-table ribbon system, eye-triangle scrap, taped labels, and drag/rotate/shuffle logic.
- The physical material layer: torn-paper section and image masks, torn edges, tape, paper scraps, xerox/toner/paper textures, halftone, ink dropout, scratches, and hand-drawn red/black marks in `assets/paper/`, `assets/textures/`, `assets/illustrations/`, and `assets/overlays/`.

- The three original ink plates: `assets/svg/plate-hand.svg`, `assets/svg/plate-wheel.svg`, and `assets/svg/plate-spiral.svg`.

- The audio synthesis, the receiver interaction, and the synthesized room tone.

## Cultural references

Appendix A contains nine textual allusions to widely circulated works:

- *The Matrix* (1999) — the unlabeled capsule.
- *Portal* (2007) — the cake that was promised.
- *2001: A Space Odyssey* (1968) — the monolith.
- *It* (1986 / 2017) — the red balloon.
- *Alice's Adventures in Wonderland* (1865) — the white rabbit.
- *Neuromancer* (1984) — the dead channel.
- *Twin Peaks* (1990) — the red room.
- *Ringu* (1998) — the VHS tape.
- *Black Mirror* (2011) — the black mirror.

The allusions are textual only. No characters, logos, footage, or original artwork from those works are reproduced. The nine relic drawings and the signal map are original to this project. The works themselves remain the property of their makers.
